# DingoOS Belief-Neutral Evidence and Mathematical Validity Policy — Progress Record
Date: 2026-10-10

## Principle clarified
DingoOS Pty Ltd IP does not use personal belief or disbelief as an evidentiary criterion. A proposition is not accepted or rejected because someone believes it, disbelieves it, feels confident about it, prefers it, or has high status.

## Operational rule
- Mathematical propositions: explicit definitions, axioms/premises, valid inference, proof or conjecture/counterexample.
- Empirical propositions: suitable observations, measurements, uncertainty, controls, predictions and replication where feasible.
- Historical/source claims: identifiable sources, provenance, context and corroboration.
- Engineering decisions: requirements, tests, hazards, operating limits, residual risks and authorization.
- Missing/conflicting evidence: preserve UNKNOWN / INCONCLUSIVE / SOURCE_NEEDED or the appropriate supported status. Absence of evidence is not automatically confirmation or refutation.

Axioms and assumptions are explicit premises within a formal system, not personal beliefs. Proof status, empirical status, maturity, operational state and authorization remain distinct.
