## Semantic–Frequency Coupling Postulate (SFCP-01)
SFCP-01 is explicitly labeled HYPOTHESIS / ACTIVE_RESEARCH, not an established theorem or law. It proposes a testable question: whether a pre-defined semantic condition adds reproducible out-of-sample predictive information about a calibrated signal or outcome beyond a baseline with controls. The document supplies candidate estimands, preregistration requirements, null controls, confounders, replication conditions, and falsification/downgrade criteria.

Ideas associated with Bashar and Elizabeth April are handled as source-attributed conceptual prompts or experiential reports—not evidence of channeling, psychic abilities, extraterrestrial communication, metaphysical mechanisms, or consciousness-mediated physics. Reports, interpretations, hypotheses, predictions, test results, and evidence decisions are kept separate.
