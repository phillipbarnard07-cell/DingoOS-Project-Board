## Design invariants
- Do not restart the project or duplicate canonical registries/ledgers.
- DESIGNED, IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, INTEGRATED, VERIFIED, AUTHORIZED and DEPLOYED/MONITORED remain separate.
- Missing execution logs or exact-revision evidence means UNKNOWN/HOLD, never PASS.
- Synthetic, simulated, observed and independently replicated results remain distinguishable.
- Free-first development; no paid API/CI/billing gates without explicit owner approval.
- Private IP/security details remain in the private repository; the public board contains sanitized progress only.
