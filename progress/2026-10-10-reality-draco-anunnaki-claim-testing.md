## Epistemic safeguards

- Mathematical consistency is not empirical proof.
- Anunnaki in ancient Mesopotamian traditions does not by itself establish extraterrestrial identity or engineering of humans.
- Biblical parallels must be passage-specific and chronology-aware; similar motifs do not prove direct transmission or identity.
- Evidence for threat-related neural circuitry or the “reptilian brain” metaphor does not establish a Draco connection.
- A missing record alone does not prove deliberate suppression.
- Preserve unknown, refuted, failed, contradictory, and inconclusive outcomes.
- No paid CI, paid APIs, or billing gates introduced.
